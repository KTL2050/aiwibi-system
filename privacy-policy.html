Privacy Policy

Aiwibi — lumio.ug  |  Last updated: August 3, 2026

Aiwibi ("we," "us," "our") operates a web platform that helps mothers connect their social media accounts, view content performance analytics, and earn rewards. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide directly: name, email address, and login credentials (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Connected social media data: when you link a TikTok or Instagram account, we access data made available through those platforms' official APIs, such as your public profile information, content performance metrics (views, reach, engagement, watch time), and post metadata.
  • Usage data: how you interact with Aiwibi, including pages visited and features used, to improve the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your social media content performance analytics on your Aiwibi dashboard.
  • To calculate and administer platform rewards based on your content activity.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Aiwibi platform.
  • To communicate with you about your account or platform updates.

3. Data Obtained via TikTok and Instagram APIs

Where we access data through TikTok's or Instagram's official APIs, we do so only with your explicit authorization (OAuth), and only to power the specific features you've enabled (analytics display and rewards). Specifically:

  • We do not sell data obtained via these APIs to third parties.
  • We do not use this data for advertising purposes outside of Aiwibi.
  • We retain API-sourced data only as long as needed to provide the analytics and rewards features, or until you disconnect the linked account.
  • You can revoke Aiwibi's access to your TikTok or Instagram account at any time, either from within Aiwibi's settings or directly from your TikTok/Instagram app settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, authentication, analytics infrastructure), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and connected-account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below; we will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for connected social media accounts at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Aiwibi is intended for adult users. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated revision date.
